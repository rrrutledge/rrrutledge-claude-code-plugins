Spec title

Name the thing being proposed. Optionally follow with one sentence summarizing what it is.

Business value

Who will use this, and what end goal are they trying to accomplish? Describe the outcome they're after - the thing that provides value when they reach it.

Problem

What gets in the way of them reaching that end goal today? Describe the obstacle concretely - what they run into and what it costs them.

What we'll do

How we'll help them overcome that problem so they can reach their end goal. Describe the solution as what they'll be able to do; save implementation detail for Detailed notes.

Visuals

Diagrams, mockups, and screenshots of what it will look like. Each visual sits full width with a one-line caption saying what it shows. A flow or architecture diagram plus a mockup of the main screen usually covers it.

Detailed notes

Everything a builder or reviewer needs beyond the summary: scope in and out, options considered, dependencies, rollout phases, risks, and open questions. Sub-headings and tables work well here; this section carries whatever length the spec has.
